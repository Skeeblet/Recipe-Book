Recipe 01

Apple Cinnamon Overnight Oats

Meal-preppable, high fiber, high protein breakfast — ready to grab from the fridge every morning

Breakfast Meal prep High protein Low calorie
385Cal
20gProtein
8gFiber
3gFat
Ingredients — per jar
  • Rolled oats (not instant)½ cup
  • Nonfat Greek yogurt (Fage 0%)½ cup
  • Unsweetened almond milk¼ cup
  • Chia seeds½ tbsp
  • Sugar free vanilla syrup (Torani)½ tbsp
  • Vanilla extract¼ tsp
  • Cinnamon½ tsp
  • Medium apple, diced fresh each morning1 whole apple
Instructions
  1. 1
    Combine oats, yogurt, almond milk, chia seeds, vanilla syrup, vanilla extract, and cinnamon into your jar. Stir really well — make sure chia seeds don't clump at the bottom.
  2. 2
    Seal the jar and refrigerate overnight or at least 4 hours. The oats will absorb everything and thicken significantly.
  3. 3
    In the morning, give it a stir. If too thick, add a small splash of almond milk. Should be creamy and thick, not watery.
  4. 4
    Dice the whole apple into small chunks and add on top fresh.
Notes & tips
Apple: One whole apple per jar — buy 5 apples for a 5-day week. Always add fresh, never the night before.  |  Oats: Rolled oats only — instant turns to paste overnight.  |  Shelf life: Jars keep 5 days in the fridge. Make all 5 Sunday night. Days 4–5 may thicken — add a splash of almond milk.
Recipe 02

Veggie Egg White Omelette

Quick, high protein breakfast ready in under 10 minutes — loaded with veggies

Breakfast Quick make High protein Low calorie
135Cal
20gProtein
4gFiber
1gFat
Ingredients — 1 serving
  • Liquid egg whites (carton)¾ cup
  • Bell pepper diced small½ pepper
  • Baby spinach½ cup
  • Mushrooms sliced3 mushrooms
  • Yellow onion diced small¼ onion
  • Garlic powderpinch
  • Salt and black pepperpinch
  • Cooking sprayas needed
Instructions
  1. 1
    Spray a non-stick pan with cooking spray over medium heat. Add onion and bell pepper and cook for 2–3 minutes until slightly softened. Add mushrooms and cook another minute.
  2. 2
    Add spinach and stir until just wilted, about 30 seconds. Push all vegetables to one side of the pan.
  3. 3
    Pour egg whites over the vegetables and let spread across the pan. Season with garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Do not stir — let it set on the bottom for about 2 minutes.
  4. 4
    Once the edges look set and the top is mostly cooked through, fold one half over the other. Cook 30–60 more seconds then slide onto your plate.
Notes & tips
Egg whites: Buy liquid egg whites in a carton (Egg Beaters or store brand) — much easier than separating eggs.  |  Calorie swap: At only 135 cal this gives you 185 extra calories of flexibility elsewhere in your day.  |  Non-stick pan is essential — egg whites stick badly to stainless steel.

Recipe 03

Turkey & Veggie Stir Fry

High volume, high protein, high fiber — built for satiety on a low-calorie diet

Lunch Dinner Meal prep High protein Low calorie
272Cal
28gProtein
10gFiber
5gFat
Ingredients — 6 portions
  • Ground turkey breast 93/71.5 lbs
  • Frozen cauliflower rice6 cups (2 bags)
  • Broccoli florets6 cups
  • Shredded cabbage6 cups
  • Canned black beans (drained)1½ cups
  • Low-sodium soy sauce12 tbsp
  • Rice wine vinegar1 tbsp
  • Garlic powder1 tsp
  • Cooking sprayas needed
Instructions
  1. 1
    Spray pan over medium-high heat. Add turkey and cook until no longer pink, breaking apart. Season with garlic powder. Cook in batches — don't overcrowd.
  2. 2
    Add broccoli and cabbage. Stir fry on high heat 4–5 minutes until softened but still with a little crunch. Undercook slightly for meal prep.
  3. 3
    Add black beans, soy sauce, and rice wine vinegar. Toss and cook 2 minutes until sauce coats everything.
  4. 4
    Add cauliflower rice and fold everything together. Cook 2–3 minutes until heated through and sauce is absorbed.
Notes & tips
Cauliflower rice: Add at the end — goes mushy if overcooked.  |  Soy sauce: Taste as you go.  |  Optional finish: 3–4 drops sesame oil right before serving.  |  Shelf life: 4–5 days in airtight containers.
Recipe 04

Loaded Turkey Chili

High protein, high fiber, meal-prep friendly — big bowl under 300 calories

Lunch Dinner Meal prep High protein Low calorie
285Cal
28gProtein
10gFiber
6gFat
Ingredients — 6 portions
  • Ground turkey 93/71.5 lbs
  • Canned black beans (drained)1 cup
  • Canned kidney beans (drained)1 cup
  • Canned diced tomatoes (no salt)28 oz
  • Tomato paste2 tbsp
  • Low sodium chicken broth1 cup
  • Bell peppers diced2 peppers
  • Yellow onion diced1 onion
  • Zucchini diced1 zucchini
  • Celery stalks diced3 stalks
  • Garlic cloves minced4 cloves
  • Chili powder2 tbsp
  • Cumin1 tbsp
  • Smoked paprika1 tsp
  • Cayenne pepper½ tsp
  • Cooking sprayas needed
Instructions
  1. 1
    Spray a large pot over medium-high heat. Add turkey and cook until no longer pink, breaking apart. About 6–7 minutes. Drain excess liquid.
  2. 2
    Add onion and garlic. Cook 3–4 minutes until onion softens. Add bell peppers, zucchini, and celery and cook another 3 minutes.
  3. 3
    Add all spices directly to the mixture. Stir well and cook 1 minute to bloom the spices — this makes a huge difference in flavor.
  4. 4
    Pour in diced tomatoes, tomato paste, chicken broth, and both beans. Stir and bring to a boil.
  5. 5
    Reduce heat to low, cover partially, and simmer 25–30 minutes stirring occasionally until thickened.
  6. 6
    Taste and adjust — more heat? Add cayenne. Too thick? Add a splash more broth.
Notes & tips
Flavor tip: Tastes even better on days 2 and 3 as flavors develop.  |  Topping: Use nonfat Greek yogurt instead of sour cream — saves ~50 cal and adds protein.  |  Shelf life: 5 days in the fridge, or freeze portions for up to 3 months.
Recipe 05

BBQ Chicken Pizza

High protein, low fat — layered tortilla pizza that comes together in under 15 minutes

Lunch Dinner Quick make High protein
627Cal
100gProtein
8gFiber
9gFat
Ingredients — 1 serving
  • Ole Xtreme Wellness high fiber tortillas2 tortillas
  • BBQ sauce (G Hughes or homemade Recipe 06)5 tbsp split evenly
  • Great Value chunk chicken breast (canned, drained)4 servings split evenly
  • Heb fat free natural mozzarella cheese4 oz split evenly
  • Red pepper flakes (topper)to taste
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 425°F. Place first tortilla flat on a baking sheet or oven-safe pan.
  2. 2
    Spread half the BBQ sauce evenly over the first tortilla. Top with half the chicken and half the cheese.
  3. 3
    Layer the second tortilla on top. Spread remaining BBQ sauce, chicken, and cheese on top.
  4. 4
    Bake at 425°F for 7 minutes until cheese is melted and tortilla edges are crispy. Let sit 1–2 minutes, top with red pepper flakes, then slice.
Notes & tips
Half serving: Split into 2 meals at ~314 cal each — reheat the second half in the oven or air fryer at 375°F for 3–4 min to keep it crispy.  |  Crispier base: Place directly on the oven rack instead of a baking sheet.  |  BBQ sauce: Use Recipe 06 for a less sweet, tangier, spicier version.

Recipe 06

Homemade Tangy & Spicy BBQ Sauce

Vinegar-forward, smoky, and spicy — zero sugar, ~8 calories per tablespoon

Sauce Quick make Meal prep Low calorie
~8Cal/tbsp
0gSugar
~15Servings
$1.25Per batch
Ingredients — 1 batch (~15 tbsp)
  • Tomato paste (small can)6 oz
  • Apple cider vinegar⅓ cup
  • Water⅓ cup
  • Worcestershire sauce1 tbsp
  • Liquid smoke (hickory)1 tsp
  • Smoked paprika1 tsp
  • Garlic powder1 tsp
  • Cayenne pepper½ tsp
  • Red pepper flakes½ tsp
  • Onion powder½ tsp
  • Great Value monk fruit & erythritol sweetener1 tsp
  • Salt¼ tsp
  • Black pepper¼ tsp
Instructions
  1. 1
    Add all ingredients to a small saucepan and whisk together until smooth.
  2. 2
    Bring to medium heat until it just starts to bubble, then reduce to low. Simmer 8–10 minutes stirring occasionally until slightly thickened.
  3. 3
    Taste and adjust — more tang? Add a splash more apple cider vinegar. More heat? Add more cayenne. Too tangy? Add a tiny bit more monk fruit sweetener, a few drops at a time.
  4. 4
    Cool completely then transfer to a jar. Store in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.
Notes & tips
Cost: ~$1.25 per batch vs $3–4 for G Hughes or $10+ for specialty sugar free brands. Makes enough for 2–3 pizzas.  |  Sweetener: Use Great Value Monk Fruit & Erythritol (~$5 for 16oz at Walmart) — no bitter aftertaste unlike stevia.  |  Liquid smoke: Don't exceed 1 tsp — a little goes a very long way.  |  Tip: Flavor improves significantly after 24 hours in the fridge.
